Sub: Introduction of techKoodaram – A Volunteer Technology Community from Southern Tamil Nadu.
Respected Sir,

We write to introduce techKoodaram, a volunteer-driven technology community based in Southern Tamil Nadu. We are a group of working professionals, students, and educators who come together to learn, share knowledge, and build things — with the belief that technology should be useful and accessible to everyone.

About techKoodaram

techKoodaram was started with a simple idea: bring together people who are curious about technology and give them a space to learn and contribute. Our community has grown to over 600 members across Tamil Nadu, with a strong presence in Southern districts.

We are not a company or a paid platform. Every session, every program, and every mentorship connection we facilitate is voluntary and free of charge.

Vision

To build an inclusive technology community where every student, educator, and citizen in Southern Tamil Nadu can learn, grow, and contribute — regardless of where they are from.

Mission

  • Make technology awareness practical and accessible — not just theoretical.
  • Connect students with professionals for real mentorship and career guidance.
  • Encourage people to build things — products, projects, and solutions — together.
  • Support Tamil language participation in the AI and tech ecosystem.
  • Bring communities together to solve local problems through technology.

What We Do

We run regular community sessions — offline and online — open to anyone interested in technology. So far, we have completed 14 sessions, engaged with 3 colleges, and built a community of 600+ members across Tamil Nadu.

Our sessions run in four formats:

  • Tech Talk — latest trends in technology, followed by open discussion and networking.
  • Builder Session — community members come together to build a software product as a group.
  • Product Showcase — individuals and teams pitch their side project ideas to the community.
  • Meet Your Co-Founder — founders share their startup ideas and invite interested members to join them.

How techKoodaram Can Help Virudhunagar District

Virudhunagar has colleges, students, and a motivated young population. What many of them lack is access to practical technology exposure, mentorship, and a community that encourages them to build and innovate. techKoodaram can help fill that gap — as a volunteer community, at no cost.

  • Technology and AI Awareness Sessions — We can conduct sessions in colleges and schools to help students and teachers understand AI and emerging technologies in a practical, hands-on way.
  • Student Mentorship and Career Guidance — Our members are working professionals who are willing to guide students on careers in technology, help them build skills, and connect them to real opportunities.
  • Tamil and AI — We encourage Tamil speakers to participate in the AI ecosystem and create awareness about how AI can be used in the Tamil language context.
  • Community Innovation — We can organise collaborative sessions where students and professionals work together on local problem statements — encouraging innovation rooted in real needs.
  • Volunteer Technology Network — Our members are willing to contribute their time and skills to any district program where technology awareness or digital participation can add value.

Immediate Opportunities for Collaboration

  • Facilitate introduction to interested government schools and colleges in the district.
  • Identify district programs where technology volunteers can meaningfully contribute.
  • Enable periodic engagement with educational institutions under district administration.

Our Long-Term Vision for Virudhunagar

Our long-term vision is to help position Virudhunagar District as a model district for technology awareness, student innovation, and responsible AI adoption.

We sincerely thank the District Collector for taking the time to read this letter. We would be happy to meet and share more about our work whenever convenient.
